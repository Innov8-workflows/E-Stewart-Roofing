Roofing in Prestwick

Prestwick is coastal and low-lying, so it gets the same salt-laden westerlies as the rest of the Ayrshire shore with very little shelter. The housing is dominated by interwar and post-war bungalows and villas, a great many of them roughcast, which means external wall work is as much a part of the job here as the roof above it. Roughcast on an exposed elevation blows over time, and once it does the wall behind it starts taking water it was never meant to hold.

Whatever is on your roof, the job starts the same way: someone actually looks at it and tells you what is wrong. If a repair will see you right for a few more years, that is what you will be told.

Roofing in Glasgow

Glasgow has one of the largest concentrations of Victorian and Edwardian tenement housing anywhere in Britain, and tenements bring their own roofing problems: shared valleys and party walls, substantial stacks sitting on the boundary between two properties, and access that has to be worked out before anything else can happen. Blond and red sandstone with natural slate is the dominant traditional combination, and on those roofs it is usually the fixings and the lead that fail rather than the slate itself. Further out the housing shifts to interwar and post-war semis on tile, where ridge mortar and tired rainwater goods are the recurring jobs.

Whatever is on your roof, the job starts the same way: someone actually looks at it and tells you what is wrong. If a repair will see you right for a few more years, that is what you will be told.
